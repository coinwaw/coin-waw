# Coin Waw Roadmap

> Build a useful financial information community first. Let the token support the community rather than replace the product.

Roadmap items are goals and may change based on technical feasibility, costs, community feedback, data licensing, and available resources.

---

## What We Will Not Promise

Coin Waw will not use the roadmap to imply guaranteed:

- token price
- returns
- market capitalization
- exchange listings
- liquidity
- partnerships
- future utility

Features are considered complete only when they are actually implemented.
