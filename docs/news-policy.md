# Coin Waw News Policy

## Purpose

This document explains how Coin Waw approaches financial news.

## Source Priority

### Tier 1 — Primary Sources

- government agencies
- central banks
- public-company filings
- official company announcements
- official blockchain/project announcements

### Tier 2 — Established Financial Media

Recognized financial and business publications.

### Tier 3 — Secondary Sources

Social media posts, analysts, commentators, and community sources.

Tier 3 information should be treated carefully and should not be presented as confirmed fact without verification.

## Verification

For major breaking news, contributors should attempt to verify:

- who made the claim
- original source
- publication time
- whether credible sources confirm it
- whether information is official or speculative

## Corrections

If Coin Waw publishes incorrect information:

1. Correct the original post where possible.
2. Clearly identify the correction.
3. Provide the correct source.
4. Avoid silently changing material information.

## Headlines

Headlines should be:

- concise
- factual
- understandable
- non-misleading

Avoid unnecessary clickbait.

## Commentary

Commentary must be distinguishable from factual reporting.

**FACT**

> The central bank announced X.

**COMMENTARY**

> This could increase volatility in risk assets.

The second statement is interpretation, not a confirmed fact.
