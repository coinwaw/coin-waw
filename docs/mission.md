# Coin Waw Mission

## Mission

Coin Waw exists to make financial information easier to discover, understand, and discuss.

Financial markets produce an enormous amount of information every day.

The challenge is often not finding information. It is finding the signal.

Coin Waw aims to build a community that tracks the stories behind market movements.

## Vision

Build an independent financial information community combining:

- news
- market data
- research
- technology
- community

## Coverage

### Crypto
Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, altcoins, protocols, regulation, adoption, and blockchain technology.

### Stocks
Public companies, earnings, technology companies, corporate events, and major equity-market developments.

### Macro
Interest rates, inflation, central banks, employment, liquidity, currencies, commodities, and global economic developments.

## What We Don't Do

Coin Waw does not promise financial returns.

We do not intentionally present speculation as fact.

We do not intentionally create fake news.

We do not guarantee token performance.

## Long-Term Goal

Build something that remains useful even when market hype disappears.

If Coin Waw succeeds, people should follow it because the information is useful—not simply because they own $WAW.
